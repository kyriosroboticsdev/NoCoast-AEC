- Minimums: WC/powder room 1.0 × 1.6 m (toilet + washbasin); shower room 1.8 × 2.2 m; family bathroom 2.2 × 2.8 m.
- Keep all wet bricks on one or two walls so they share a drain: toilet and washbasin on one wall (different `at`),
  shower or bathtub on the adjacent wall.
- `toilet` needs 0.6 m clear in front and should not face the door directly. `bathtub` runs along a full wall
  (side N/S/E/W, at 0.5) — it is 1.7 m long, so check the wall is ≥ 2.0 m.
- `walk_in_shower` for accessible or luxury bathrooms; add a `floor_drain` in it (same side/at).
- Every bathroom gets an `extractor_fan` (ceiling) — more so when it has no window — and a `towel_radiator`
  if the building has heating (a heat pump or boiler).
- Stack bathrooms above each other on multi-storey buildings: it shortens the drain riser.
- En-suites share a wall with their bedroom and have a door to it, not to the hall.
