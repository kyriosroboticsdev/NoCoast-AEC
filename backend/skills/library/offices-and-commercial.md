- Open-plan office: `workstation` benches (1.6 m per person) in rows 1.8 m apart, `office_chair` per person,
  `partition_screen`s between teams. ≈ 10 m² per person including circulation.
- Meeting rooms: `meeting_table` centred, 1.0 m clear around. Reception: `reception_desk` facing the entrance door.
- Data: a `network_rack` in a comms/storage room, `wifi_access_point` per ~80 m², `printer` near the hall.
- Offices are ventilated mechanically: `air_handling_unit` (plant room) or `rooftop_unit`, `supply_diffuser`s ≈ one
  per 15 m² and `return_grille`s.
- Apply the fire-safety skill's non-domestic rules (call points, sounders, emergency lights, exit signs).
