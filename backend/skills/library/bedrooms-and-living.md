- Beds: headboard against a solid wall (not the window wall when avoidable), 0.6–0.7 m walkway on both long sides
  of a double. `nightstand` bricks go on the same wall, at the bed's at ± 0.25.
- `wardrobe` on a wall without a window; keep 0.8 m in front. Children's rooms: `single_bed` or `bunk_bed`, `desk`.
- Living rooms: `sofa` facing the `tv_stand` or `television` (opposite walls); `coffee_table` centred between them;
  `rug` under the coffee table (rugs may overlap). A `corner_sofa` needs ≥ 14 m².
- Dining: `dining_table` or `round_dining_table` centred, 0.9 m clear on every side for chairs; one `dining_chair`
  per 0.6 m of table edge (place them with near points around the table). `pendant_light` over the table.
- Every habitable room: a window, a door, and — when the brief mentions them — `double_socket`s on two walls.
