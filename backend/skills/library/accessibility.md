- Step-free entrance: an `access_ramp` (1:12 — 0.5 m rise needs d = 6 m) outside the entrance door, position so its
  top end meets the door; or make the entrance level.
- Multi-storey: a `passenger_elevator` in the hall, at the SAME position on every storey it serves (one brick per
  level), 1.5 m clear in front. `stair_lift` only as a retrofit along an existing stair.
- Doors 0.9–1.0 m wide (door width), halls ≥ 1.2 m wide, 1.5 m turning circles in bathrooms and kitchens.
- Accessible bathroom on the entrance storey: `walk_in_shower`, `wall_hung_wc` with a `grab_bar` beside it (same wall
  side, at ± 0.15), basin with knee space.
- `handrail`s (span) along both sides of stairs and ramps.
