- `skylight` (host roof) over rooms with little facade — deep halls, bathrooms, stairwells on the top storey.
  Give its `position` inside the room below.
- Overheating: `brise_soleil` over large south/west windows (same wall side, mount above the window), `awning`
  over patio doors, `exterior_shutter`s either side of bedroom windows.
- A `fireplace` or `wood_stove` needs a `flue_chimney` — put the chimney against the same wall and add it on every
  storey above so it reaches the roof.
- Flat roofs: `roof_drain`s. Pitched roofs shed water to gutters (no bricks needed).
