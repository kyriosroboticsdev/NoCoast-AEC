- Homes: a `smoke_detector` on every hall/landing ceiling and in every bedroom and living room; a `heat_detector`
  in the kitchen and garage (never smoke there); a `co_detector` in rooms with a boiler, stove or fireplace.
- Sprinklers: `sprinkler_head`s on the ceiling of every room, about one per 12 m² (position them on a 3–3.5 m grid),
  plus ONE `sprinkler_valve_set` in the utility/plant room — without it the heads have no water.
- Non-domestic buildings (offices, shops, schools): `manual_call_point` beside each exit door, a
  `fire_alarm_sounder` per floor, `emergency_light` along escape routes, an `exit_sign` above every exit door,
  a `fire_extinguisher` near each exit. `fire_hose_reel` for large floor plates (> 500 m²).
- Every storey needs a way out: a stair (or the ground-floor entrance); upper storeys of more than two need a
  second stair or a protected hall.
